We usually reply within a few days. Please include your phone model and what happened.

I bought something and don't have it

Open the Herd tab, scroll to the Ranch Store, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

I got a new phone or reinstalled the game

Purchases come back with Restore purchases. Your ranch itself is saved only on your phone (the game has no accounts or cloud saves), so a new install starts a new ranch.

Ads

Ads are optional: they only play when you tap a "Watch a short ad" button. The Ranch Pass skips every ad and keeps the rewards. Players in some regions can change their ad choices under Herd → Settings → Privacy choices.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores lives on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it. See the privacy policy for what our partners (ads, purchases, updates) receive.
