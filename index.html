Herdfinity is a cattle ranching game. This policy explains what information the game and its partners collect, and why.

What the game itself stores

  • Your ranch save (herd, cash, upgrades, settings) is stored on your device only. We do not run servers that receive it.
  • The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads. These are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID)
  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location
  • ad interactions and app diagnostics

This information is used to deliver, measure and, where you have agreed, personalize ads. Players in the EEA, UK and certain US states are asked for consent first and can change their choice anytime under Herd → Settings → Privacy choices. On iOS you can also allow or deny tracking in the system prompt or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Owners of the Ranch Pass never see ads.

In-app purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm and restore purchases. It receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this game, device type and country. See RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

Herdfinity is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content rated PG or below.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in Herd → Settings → Privacy choices (where offered).
  • Reset your advertising ID or limit tracking in your device settings.
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
